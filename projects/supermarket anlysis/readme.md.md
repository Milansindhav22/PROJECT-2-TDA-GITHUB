\# Supermarket Sales Analysis

\## 📋 Project Overview This project analyzes supermarket sales data to
identify patterns in customer behavior, determine best-selling products,
and track sales trends over time.

\## 🎯 Objectives 1. Analyze daily sales patterns and trends 2. Identify
top-performing product lines 3. Examine customer demographics and
purchasing behavior 4. Provide actionable insights for inventory and
marketing

\## 📊 Dataset - \*\*Source:\*\* \`supermarket_sales.csv\` -
\*\*Records:\*\* 1000 transactions - \*\*Features:\*\* Invoice ID,
Branch, City, Customer type, Gender, Product line, Unit price, Quantity,
Tax, Total, Date, Time, Payment, COGS, Gross margin percentage, Gross
income, Rating

\## 🔍 Key Findings 1. \*\*Fashion accessories\*\* and \*\*Food &
beverages\*\* are the top-selling categories 2. Sales show weekly
patterns with higher revenue on weekends 3. Gender distribution is
relatively balanced with slight variations by product category 4.
Average transaction value: \~\$320

\## 📈 Visualizations Generated - \`daily_sales_trend.png\` - Time
series of daily sales - \`top_products.png\` - Bar chart of best-selling
product lines - \`gender_distribution.png\` - Pie chart of sales by
gender
